Search vs Locate

  1. Search: you hold a tag → app tells you what product it is.
  2. Shows SKU, EAN, USN, location, status (stock / sold / transit).
  3. For sets: parent SKU + sibling child tags.

Locate is the reverse: SKU → list of all tagged units.
